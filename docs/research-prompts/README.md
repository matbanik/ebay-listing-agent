# Research prompts

The deep-research prompts that shaped the pipeline's first design, one per model. They are kept as a record of the questions asked, not as current instructions.

They predate later changes. The prompts describe listing through the `ebay-mcp` server; the pipeline now calls eBay through `core/tools/ebay_api.py`, and `core/AGENTS.md` describes how it works today. The research answers themselves are not included.
